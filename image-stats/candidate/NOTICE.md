The Smaug adapter reproduces preprocessing and inference from Abacus.AI's
Apache-2.0 `Smaug-AI-Image-Detector` wrapper at revision
`c7c95951e04a87b884a4ad479401b1128c3903bd`.

Source: https://huggingface.co/abacusai/Smaug-AI-Image-Detector

The model weights are downloaded from that pinned repository, not stored in
this dataset. The [Apache-2.0 license](../src/image_stats/authenticity/SMAUG-LICENSE.txt)
is included with the adapter. Source-photo licenses and attribution evidence
are preserved per image in `records/`; generated images retain their exact
provider/model/prompt records in `generation.json`.
