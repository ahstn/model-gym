# Sol red-team: Astra and Task plans

**Verdict:** fund the local 2B decision-readout program, with a conditional 4B comparison; do not preauthorize 12B research or 27B training. The live board is v0.2.1 (Jev 57.89, AutoJev 56.40, Decider 2B 28.97, Decider 4B 40.70), whereas the runnable public kit is v0.2. These are different objectives; reconstructed aggregate arithmetic is not proof of identical row construction or scoring. Use a pinned official 0.2 run and independently held-out source-family panels, label any 0.2.1 reconstruction accordingly. [Leaderboard audit](../research/leaderboard.md), [public kit](https://github.com/apolinario/decision-index), [live methodology](https://huggingface.co/spaces/multimodalart/jev-decision-index/resolve/main/data/methodology.json).

## Astra: what to reject

- **E3 equivariant candidate-set readout: reject as a core experiment.** Branching every candidate through a 2.5B decoder repeats candidate-prefix work K times until a nontrivial shared-prefix implementation works, then still pays candidate-token and quadratic set-attention costs at K=32–255. Independent candidate branches also remove cross-candidate language-model interaction before the tiny set head; two 256-dimensional blocks are not evidence that useful listwise reasoning survives. Its 24–48 local GPU-hour forecast excludes substantial implementation/debugging and expanded-token cost. A formal 99.5% permutation agreement is low commercial value if paired gold accuracy or 255-option latency suffers. **Replacement:** shuffle options during conventional one-slot SFT, measure remapped TV, paired accuracy and p95 at K=2/8/32/255; test two-order averaging *only* for an observed order-sensitive segment. Authorize a 5k-row head-only set-scoring pilot only if a consequential residual order error survives and estimated branch cost meets the product SLA. This is a recommendation based on [Astra E3](../plans/plan-astra.md), [Sol E5](../plans/plan-sol.md), and the established one-slot competitors [Winnow audit](../research/competitor-winnow.md).
- **E8 27B challenger: delete from approved budget.** 27B LoRA on 96GB plus conditional 8×H100 full FT (Astra estimates $383–766 GPU-only for full FT) is a different capital allocation, not a natural 2B/4B continuation. Around 16 bytes/parameter already implies ~445GB for 27.78B Adam FT before activations and snapshots; 60GB host RAM/440GB free local disk cannot serve as a casual optimizer/checkpoint backstop. A 12B gain on development does not establish 27B clean-transfer benefit or on-3090 deployment. **Replacement:** rent one short BF16 *stock 27B inference* comparator only if the local baseline leaves an identified, consequential capability gap; no 27B training absent a separately approved product and cash case. [Astra E8](../plans/plan-astra.md), [memory audit](../research/hw-3090.md), [AutoJev audit](../research/competitor-autojev.md).
- E0 rents a 96GB GPU before the local 2B/4B floor exists; E2 trains two encoders and a cross-encoder despite the encoder tier topping at 14.32; E4 adds two teacher families, rationale generation and controls; E6 learns a router before a cost-justified 12B endpoint exists; E7 truncates depth before prefill is shown to dominate. These form a research portfolio, not the minimum route to a working decision model. Keep one encoder operational control, and defer teachers, cascade and depth ablations until measured failure and latency traces specify their payoff. [Astra ladder](../plans/plan-astra.md), [tier board](../research/leaderboard.md), [Sol ladder](../plans/plan-sol.md).
- **Timeline arithmetic is not credible as a two-week promise.** Astra's medium envelope itself allocates 210 local GPU-hours plus 158 rental hours, before harness engineering, 200k-row provenance review, 255-token-code validation, annotation, checkpoint transfer, calibration and a 120,340-request full suite. Its Day 5 27B teacher audit precedes the Day 6–7 training floor; Day 9–10 E3 requires a new branch model and three pilots; Day 14 final results presume source-family sealed tests and public edition parity already exist. The 3090's ~2.5k tok/s 2B and ~1.25k tok/s 4B estimates are theoretical rather than benchmarks; 40M training tokens alone take ~4.4/8.9 hours at these rates before eval/retries, and a 160M 4B expansion ~35.6 hours before overhead. Use a warmed 100–500-step actual-token pilot; milestones are event-gated rather than calendar commitments. [Astra schedule](../plans/plan-astra.md), [3090 throughput note](../research/hw-3090.md). The official MiniCPM recipe supports BF16 r16 LoRA at 2048 tokens, but its 200-sample demonstration runtime is not evidence for a 40–200M-token project ETA: [MiniCPM recipe](https://github.com/OpenBMB/MiniCPM/blob/main/docs/finetune/trl.md).
- Astra's $700 medium cap has $250 annotation/data and $190 contingency while running all E0–E7; cash discipline says first get an independently scored 2B result with $0 rental. Its p95 512-token <=200ms target is an unmeasured hard gate that risks rejecting a useful model by assertion; measure end-to-end 3090 and actual competing solution under comparable load. [Astra budgets](../plans/plan-astra.md), [Sol budgets](../plans/plan-sol.md).

## Task: what to reject

- **Two teachers are not independent truth.** Stock Qwen3.6-27B Decider-chat and AutoJev-27B share a Qwen-family foundation; AutoJev has public benchmark checkpoint-selection exposure. Averaging their calibrated candidate probabilities produces correlated errors, not a human-vote distribution, and an agreement-only filter systematically drops genuinely ambiguous/hard cases. The Task proposal labels 150k prompts twice, generates 45k states, audits 300 per family, and assumes 22 PRO6000 hours; 150k×2×600 input tokens = 180M input tokens *before* additional reasoning/decode, teacher logits, prompt generation, setup and auditing. At 22h the required sustained rate is ~2,273 input tokens/s for two 27B passes with no overhead. This is **unverified**, not a credible authorization number. **Replacement:** no teacher in initial train. First run a 300–1,000 item independently gold-adjudicated, source-disjoint teacher comparison on the *observed* 2B error slices; at most one teacher and 2–5k targeted labels if it demonstrably lowers held-out gold NLL/Brier versus adding verified synthetic gold. Keep disagreement with provenance, never silently delete it. [Task teachers/E-T](../plans/plan-task.md), [AutoJev exposure](../research/leaderboard.md), [dataset provenance](../research/datasets.md).
- **Proxy-to-index regression is the wrong validation gate.** Evaluating >=10 public models on a new panel (some 9–12B, many distinct prompts/quantization), fitting known public-index scores and demanding Spearman >=.80 adds download/inference cost and selection pressure toward the public suite. Ten highly related models do not validate out-of-family predictions; a regression trained on board scores is not a sealed-test estimate. Stop-training-if-proxy-fails also makes experimental progress contingent on a fragile meta-model. **Replacement:** use development task-macro NLL, paired gold accuracy and safety cost directly; one stock-base readout baseline under identical inputs, held-out families, and a one-shot pinned public edition only after freeze. No proxy-predicted board score in trial gates. [Task E1/§3.5](../plans/plan-task.md), [board exposure caveat](../research/leaderboard.md).
- **Task's local training arithmetic silently assumes ideal throughput:** its 60k×400-token 2B pilot is 24M tokens/~2.7h at 2.5k tok/s, leaving no room for checkpointing, tokenization, effective sequence padding or eval. E8's 120M 4B tokens/~27h at 1.25k tok/s is an *ideal* floor (~26.7h); calling it a predicted full-run wall clock is misleading. E4 compares two bases plus full FT and three seeds in 20 hours; E3 three readouts in 12 hours; E5 several three-seed arms in 30 hours. Its ~290 local hours consume ~12.1 uninterrupted calendar days *just on the GPU*, so a two-week implementation schedule with data curation and scorer construction is not credible. [Task ladder](../plans/plan-task.md), [throughput assumptions](../research/hw-3090.md).
- **Full FT 2B is not a free comparison.** Conventional Adam mixed precision is ~32–40GB for 2–2.5B parameters before activations; 8-bit/bf16 optimizer can fit locally but changes the optimizer comparison and can become memory/throughput constrained. Task's 4B full FT on A100 plus six leave-one-group-out runs, a 300k-row hard-refinement stage, a 30M router, premature 75%-depth healing and RL arm are low-information until simple LoRA with verified data succeeds. Two public-index editions cannot be made equivalent by fitting three published aggregate scores to ±0.01: verify per-row inclusion, native metrics and raw-rescore parity or explicitly label reconstruction. [Task E0–E17](../plans/plan-task.md), [3090 memory](../research/hw-3090.md), [edition audit](../research/leaderboard.md).
- Its promised 42-point 4B score and ECE≤.03 are aspirations, not prediction from a proxy; the current strong released 4B entry is 40.70 and larger 9.65B Lux reaches 43.49. Task's historical command-risk comparison was already inspected and cannot be a pristine selection target; use a newly reviewed frozen panel with severe false-safe costs. [Live tier results](../research/leaderboard.md), [brief](../brief.md), [Sol product gates](../plans/plan-sol.md).

## Merge from Sol; ordered first eight *authorized* trials

Before trials, repair NVML by coordinated reboot (CUDA torch was working but kernel/userspace Nvidia versions differ); create source-family manifests and a disjoint calibration/final panel; preserve the incumbent fixed-five-class classifier. Freeze tokenizer, prompt, evaluator commit and dataset SHAs. The benchmark kit requires ~7GB source downloads and ~17GB working space if/when the full official run is approved; do not treat the kit as a free Day-1 smoke test. [Local hardware](../research/hw-3090.md), [kit audit](../research/leaderboard.md), [Sol evaluation contract](../plans/plan-sol.md).

**Tier:** primary 700M–3B MiniCPM, secondary 3–10B Qwen4B *only if* same-data transfer buys >=2 source-held-out accuracy points or >=0.03 NLL while passing measured latency and risk. Encoder is a speed control. 12B is a separately approved pilot only if stock 12B on local quantized inference improves independently adjudicated held-out macro accuracy >=2 points (or gold NLL >=0.03), current 4B is genuinely capacity-limited, and deployment may use 12B. Reject 27B full FT and E3 branch/set attention. These thresholds are authorization choices, not observed gains. [Sol gates](../plans/plan-sol.md), [board tiers](../research/leaderboard.md).

**Cash cap:** $0 rental for first eight trials; authorize up to **$100 all-in incremental external cash** only after a measured need (storage/setup/GPU included, no assumption human annotation or teacher API fits it), with Lium PRO6000 $1.19–1.29/h if actually bookable or an actually quoted RunPod alternative. Stop spending if setup plus useful throughput misses a >=2x calendar-throughput improvement over local, or if 12B stock pilot fails. Owned-GPU hours, labor, electricity and independently priced human adjudication must be reported separately; $100 is *not* a total project-cost claim. Never represent advertised marketplace rate as a guaranteed checkout quote. [Sol rental audit](../research/hw-rental-sol.md), [Lium prices](https://lium.io/pricing), [RunPod GPU catalog](https://www.runpod.io/gpu-models).
