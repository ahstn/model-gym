# Surogate Rune: competitor assessment (26 September 2026)

## Identity and version trap

- The sole Rune result from the HF author search is [`surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF`](https://huggingface.co/api/models?author=surogate&search=rune&limit=50). **Its name is stale**: current `main` is Rune **v3 BF16 safetensors, not GGUF**, a `Gemma4ForConditionalGeneration` fine-tune of `google/gemma-4-26B-A4B-it`, 26.5B total parameters, eight of 128 experts active/token, vision retained, claimed 262k context, Apache-2.0 metadata. It lists 11 shards totaling 51.6 GB and explicitly says no v3 GGUF yet ([current card](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/main/README.md)). HF author search found **no separate non-GGUF sibling** ([API query](https://huggingface.co/api/models?author=surogate&search=rune&limit=50)); this is one repo overwritten in place.
- [Revision history](https://huggingface.co/api/models/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/commits/main): v1 GGUF survives at [`2a155046`](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/2a155046d99949c8d8b413e213a78b4dc724dcb6/README.md), v2 BF16 committed September 25 at `fda0a9e8`, v3 BF16 later September 25 at `bd4a7cb`, v3 image card updated September 26. **Pin a SHA when evaluating**, not `main`; the launch [blog](https://invergent.ai/blog/rune/) still describes v1 GGUF and an old benchmark suite.
- v1 GGUF archive has Q3 13.5 GB, Q4 17.0 GB, Q5 19.3 GB, Q6 22.8 GB, Q8 26.9 GB plus ~1.2-GB vision projector and 55-MB decision-prompt importance matrix ([v1 card](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/2a155046d99949c8d8b413e213a78b4dc724dcb6/README.md), [actual archive manifest](https://huggingface.co/api/models/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/tree/2a155046d99949c8d8b413e213a78b4dc724dcb6)). [INFERENCE] v1 Q4 17 GB can plausibly fit our 24-GB 3090 with a restricted context/KV cache; v1 Q6 22.8 GB cannot comfortably leave memory for KV and execution. v2/v3 BF16 need rental GPU with >52 GB usable VRAM; authors use one 96-GB RTX PRO 6000 ([v3 card](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/main/README.md)). Do not assume `hf download ...GGUF` yields deployable GGUF anymore.

## Training disclosure: what is known, what isn't

- v1 was trained using open [Surogate C++/CUDA training/serving engine](https://github.com/invergent-ai/surogate); its [v1 card](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/2a155046d99949c8d8b413e213a78b4dc724dcb6/README.md) identifies a **training-mixture decision prompt importance matrix** for GGUF quantization, not the source, size, objective, optimizer, full-fine-tune configuration or compute of initial decision training. The fact that an entire BF16 checkpoint is released does **not** establish full fine-tuning; initial v1 full-FT is [UNVERIFIED]. No evidence found for RL as the initial v1 recipe; the engine's general support for GRPO does not establish Rune's use of GRPO ([engine repository](https://github.com/invergent-ai/surogate)).
- Crucial historical v2 card discloses the improvement: **self-distillation** of Rune's own reasoning-mode probability distribution into one-pass decisions, plus gold-labeled task examples and general-skill retention rows; **241,120 rows**, rank-8 LoRA on *every linear layer including experts*, merged into BF16 weights ([v2 revision card](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/fda0a9e8de42a04e2c93b12b7a1c1c382f39c893/README.md)). Dataset identity, teacher-logit generation mechanics, loss mixture coefficients, token count, epochs, training cards/hours are [UNVERIFIED]; do not reverse-engineer fictitious precision from row count.
- Current v3 card does not explain its extra training step at all ([v3 card](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/main/README.md)). v3-v2 deltas: index +0.78 (52.61→53.39), knowledge +1.6, language +0.5, retrieval unchanged 56.7, tools +2.1, arts −0.3 ([v2 card](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/fda0a9e8de42a04e2c93b12b7a1c1c382f39c893/README.md), [v3 card](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/main/README.md)). v3 LoRA/full-FT/RL/data/compute are [UNVERIFIED].

## Readout and deployable reuse

- [Serving protocol](https://github.com/invergent-ai/surogate/blob/main/docs/inference/decisions.md): two-turn chat, fixed system instruction and JSON-serialized shared state, question, letter-labeled **option descriptions**, first answer-token softmax restricted to letter-token logits; no sampling. `choice` maps the maximal option back to caller key, `noul` takes true-option probability, `score` returns expected ordinal level. Server's `confidence` on choice is **rescaled top probability**, not the raw probability: `(max−1/n)/(1−1/n)`; distinguish this field from calibration of option probabilities. Up to 255 choices supported if every code is one tokenizer token in prompt context. Multiple questions share state prefill and fork question suffixes; images are inserted before text ([same protocol](https://github.com/invergent-ai/surogate/blob/main/docs/inference/decisions.md)).
- Reusable cheap ingredients: render our same state+typed question with **descriptive options**, train one-token constrained letters, compare ordinary label loss against **soft targets from a stronger reasoning teacher** and gold labels, retain broad-task rows; do **temperature scaling on held-out text decisions** separately from image decisions, rather than calling unscaled raw probabilities calibrated ([v2 card](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/fda0a9e8de42a04e2c93b12b7a1c1c382f39c893/README.md), [v3 calibration](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/main/README.md), [protocol](https://github.com/invergent-ai/surogate/blob/main/docs/inference/decisions.md)). [INFERENCE] The v2 LoRA-on-MoE result argues against paying for full-MoE FT merely because v1 was leaderboard-labeled full-FT; prioritizing cheap LoRA experiments is defensible, but no open v1 recipe proves exactly what made its baseline succeed.
- For quantized baseline on 3090, explicitly fetch **historical SHA's** Q4 file (and optional vision projector only if assessing images). Validate custom prompt/template, letter tokens, exact logits readout and ECE; generic GGUF chat text generations do not reproduce the decision API or author leaderboard ([v1 quantized card](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/2a155046d99949c8d8b413e213a78b4dc724dcb6/README.md), [protocol](https://github.com/invergent-ai/surogate/blob/main/docs/inference/decisions.md)). Before serving v3 at load, note v3 card's surogate 1.5.3 MoE-router illegal-memory-access warning and claimed main-branch fix [PR 217](https://github.com/invergent-ai/surogate/pull/217); independent operational verification is [UNVERIFIED].

## Trial recommendations

1. **Now, zero rental cost:** pin v1 historical Q4 GGUF and run a tiny controlled readout of our command-risk labels (including permutation of candidate order), tracking accuracy, latency and raw-letter ECE; measure whether the model beats our smaller model on actual workload ([v1 files](https://huggingface.co/api/models/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/tree/2a155046d99949c8d8b413e213a78b4dc724dcb6), [decision protocol](https://github.com/invergent-ai/surogate/blob/main/docs/inference/decisions.md)).
2. **Train small before MoE rental:** baseline one-token typed decisions with descriptive options; LoRA on 2–4B dense base and compare gold labels vs teacher-soft-label self-distillation, retaining control tasks; calibrate on disjoint tasks. This extrapolates the disclosed v2 recipe, not an asserted v3 recipe ([v2 card](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/fda0a9e8de42a04e2c93b12b7a1c1c382f39c893/README.md)).
3. **Rent only for a decisive comparator:** run pinned v3 BF16 on ≥80/96 GB GPU only after smaller trials justify it; compare same-index-version and fixed-protocol results with live independent v3 board entry, never marketing older-suite 57.24 against newer-suite 53.39 or live 57.44 ([v3 card](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/main/README.md), [leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/multimodalart/jev-decision-index)).
