## 2. Hardware constants
- RTX 3090: ~71 TFLOPS dense BF16/FP16 tensor (FP32 accum on GeForce ≈ 35.6 for FP16-acc-FP32? — GeForce Ampere halves FP32-accum rate) [UNVERIFIED exact; Ampere GA102 whitepaper], 936 GB/s, 24 GB. No FP8, FA2 yes, FA3 no (local `model-gym info` note).
- H100 SXM: ~989 TFLOPS dense BF16, 3.35 TB/s [UNVERIFIED here; NVIDIA datasheet].
- RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell 96 GB: ~2× 3090-to-4090-class… treat as ~400–500 dense BF16 TFLOPS, FP8/FP4 support, 1.8 TB/s [UNVERIFIED].
- Realistic effective training throughput on 3090 with HF/Unsloth: **~25–35 TFLOPS** (35–50% MFU of 71) [INFERENCE]; QLoRA dequant costs another ~20–40% [INFERENCE].
- Empirical: 4090 prefill ≈3.5× 3090 (2,573 vs 9,121 tok/s at same quant) — https://specpicks.com/reviews/rtx-3090-vs-rtx-4090-llm-inference-2026

## 3. Memory budget (seq 2048 / 4096, batch 1, grad checkpointing, FA2, chunked CE)
Rules: full FT AdamW mixed = 16 B/param; pure-BF16 weights+grads + 8-bit Adam ≈ 6–8 B/param; LoRA-BF16 ≈ 2 B/param frozen; QLoRA NF4 ≈ 0.55–0.6 B/param. Activations w/ checkpointing ≈ L·seq·d·2 B (+ logits: vocab×seq×4 B unless chunked; 152K vocab × 4096 ≈ 2.5 GB fp32).
Unsloth reference: 27B QLoRA ≈ 22 GB, LoRA-16bit ≈ 64 GB (https://unsloth.ai/docs/get-started/fine-tuning-for-beginners/unsloth-requirements); Qwen3.8-27B QLoRA working set 17–19 GB on 24 GB card (https://runaihome.com/blog/fine-tune-qwen38-27b-unsloth-rtx-4090-2026/); QLoRA floors 8B 6 GB, 9B 6.5 GB, 14B 8.5 GB (https://localaimaster.com/blog/unsloth-desktop-guide). MiniCPM official recipe: BF16, r16 LoRA, grad ckpt, 2048 seq (brief; https://github.com/OpenBMB/MiniCPM/blob/main/docs/finetune/trl.md [UNVERIFIED loaded]).

## 6. Conclusions
- **Fits & practical locally**: all 0.4B–2B work (incl. full FT of 2B with 8-bit Adam), LoRA on 4B / 5.06B (K2-Horizon) at ≤4k, short ablations (≤200M tok) up to 5B, QLoRA smoke tests for 9–12B, evals for ≤9B.
- **3090 becomes the bottleneck**: (a) any full FT ≥4B (memory); (b) ≥9B at >100M tokens (week+ runs); (c) 27B dense and all MoE (memory + no FP8 + weak bnb MoE support); (d) full-suite eval of ≥12B models; (e) runs >1B tokens at any size ≥2B.
- Rent for: full-FT 4–5B or LoRA 12–27B → 1× H100/H200 or RTX PRO 6000 (96 GB lets 27B LoRA-BF16 and matches leaderboard eval hardware).
