# Top versus middle: evidence for first decision-model trials (26 September 2026)

**Version warning.** The shared brief describes the older Decision Index 0.2 snapshot (Jev 51.7, AutoJev 50.9, Rune v1 47.2). The [live index.json](https://huggingface.co/spaces/multimodalart/jev-decision-index/resolve/main/data/index.json) and [methodology.json](https://huggingface.co/spaces/multimodalart/jev-decision-index/resolve/main/data/methodology.json) now identify v0.2.1: 120,340 requests, 43 benchmarks, 38 headline panel tasks, 56 open entrants, and a changed panel/weighting. Current index score is `balanced_skill`, NOT `balanced_raw` nor old v0.2 skill; Jev is 57.89 and Rune v3 57.44. Thus never compare a v0.2 number with a v0.2.1 number or claim improvement from the numerical shift. Rune's [current card](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/main/README.md) still presents its separately conducted v0.2 run and older v1 leaderboard ranking; the live index lists v3, not v1. ## What score does and does not support

1. **Scale matters, but is not sufficient.** [Index](https://huggingface.co/spaces/multimodalart/jev-decision-index/resolve/main/data/index.json) places top 27B dense / 26B MoE at 49–57 skill; 4B trained entrants at ~35–41 and 2B at ~25–29. Yet 27B Solomon scores 36.43 and 35B Xor 41.48, below 12B Winnow 50.02; data, protocol, baseline and implementation dominate a simplistic parameter law. [INFERENCE] Start cheap and scale only after seeing transfer gains.
2. **Dense versus MoE: no controlled answer.** [Rune 26B-A4B v3](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/main/README.md) leads at 57.44, but [Decider 35B-A3B](https://huggingface.co/Mapika/decider-35b-a3b-nvfp4/raw/main/README.md) 47.11 and [Xor](https://huggingface.co/juspay/xor/raw/main/README.md) 41.48 trail [dense AutoJev](https://huggingface.co/denis-pplx/autojev-27b/raw/main/README.md) 56.40. [INFERENCE] MoE is not a reliable first investment given total weight residency, routing/runtime compatibility and confounded data.
3. **Full FT versus LoRA is confounded.** [Index](https://huggingface.co/spaces/multimodalart/jev-decision-index/resolve/main/data/index.json) shows 12B Winnow LoRA 50.02 > 35B Decider full-checkpoint 47.11, while 27B AutoJev full SFT 56.40. The index labels released artifact, not exact optimization; 4B Decider shipped a merged LoRA stage. [INFERENCE] LoRA first on 24 GB hardware, reserve full-weight training for demonstrated gains on fixed split.
4. **Diverse verifiable labels + realistic runtime questions are promising, not proven causal.** [AutoJev](https://huggingface.co/denis-pplx/autojev-27b/raw/main/README.md) has 73k curated examples; [Decider 4B](https://huggingface.co/Mapika/decider-4b/raw/main/README.md) uses 742M tokens and 29,325 hard/replay rows; [Winnow](https://huggingface.co/EldanRing/Winnow-12B/raw/main/README.md) uses teacher+verified+contrastive private mixtures; [JevK5](https://huggingface.co/alibiserikbay/JevK5/raw/main/README.md) uses 17k teacher questions. More tokens do not imply higher skill across architectures. [INFERENCE] Cover polarity, negation, unseen schemas, option order, no-evidence and ordinal score; keep source-disjoint holdouts to avoid training/evaluation overlap.
5. **Readout and calibration are different levers.** High scorers use constrained answer-token logits or one-pass heads rather than free-text parsing ([AutoJev](https://huggingface.co/denis-pplx/autojev-27b/raw/main/README.md), [Decider](https://huggingface.co/Mapika/decider-4b/raw/main/README.md), [Winnow](https://huggingface.co/EldanRing/Winnow-12B/raw/main/README.md)); nevertheless trained-logit approach is not sufficient: 27B Solomon 36.43. Temperature is typically cheap and changes probabilities without changing argmax ([Rune](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/main/README.md), [Decider](https://huggingface.co/Mapika/decider-4b/raw/main/README.md)); [index](https://huggingface.co/spaces/multimodalart/jev-decision-index/resolve/main/data/index.json) shows excellent ECE for weaker models (Xor .015 at skill 41.48) and weak ECE for strong models (Rune .120 at 57.44). Never optimize skill by ECE alone.

## Five highest-leverage first-trial levers (cost-aware priority)

1. **Establish a stock-base, zero-training constrained-logit baseline**, comparing at least one locally runnable 2–4B model with a stronger remotely evaluated 9–12B contender on the *same* typed schemas, candidate order variants, Score/Noul and source-disjoint domain set. [Decider chat's stock 27B 51.35 and Jevfire 49.37](https://huggingface.co/spaces/multimodalart/jev-decision-index/resolve/main/data/index.json) show inference engineering has real ceiling; [INFERENCE] this prevents spending GPU time to rediscover free stock performance.
2. **Train a narrow 2–4B LoRA on high-quality, diverse typed examples** (public gold + programmatically checkable tasks, source-disjoint evaluation, balanced choice/score/noul, description-following). Compare same base/no-train to LoRA to distinguish data effect from model scale; use [Decider 4B's documented multi-source/replay example](https://huggingface.co/Mapika/decider-4b/raw/main/README.md) as a pattern, not a mandate to copy 742M tokens. [INFERENCE]
3. **Standardize answer-slot candidate-token logit scoring**, field normalization, multicandidate handling and option-order counterfactual checks; measure accuracy + NLL/Brier + ECE and throughput on the same model before adding bespoke heads. Published [Winnow](https://huggingface.co/EldanRing/Winnow-12B/raw/main/README.md), [Decider](https://huggingface.co/Mapika/decider-4b/raw/main/README.md) and [Xor](https://huggingface.co/juspay/xor/raw/main/README.md) demonstrate implementable readouts; [INFERENCE] avoiding autoregressive answers simplifies typed-probability correctness.
4. **Fit held-out temperature(s) after freezing weights**; compare one scalar versus three field-type scalars by held-out NLL and Brier, report raw and calibrated separately and verify rank/argmax stability. [Decider 4B](https://huggingface.co/Mapika/decider-4b/raw/main/README.md) reports per-type improvement while [Solomon](https://huggingface.co/DoccyHealth/Solomon/raw/main/README.md) rejected its per-type fit; avoid assuming one solution generalizes. [INFERENCE]
5. **Escalate hardware/model size only on measured source-disjoint gain per dollar**: compare 2B→4B on local 3090 after driver works; rent 48–80+ GB only for a pre-registered 9–12B LoRA or stock 27B inference experiment if the 4B is inadequate. [Winnow's 12B Q8 artifact is 11.80 GiB](https://huggingface.co/EldanRing/Winnow-12B/raw/main/README.md), so a quantized stock comparator may be locally practical; [Rune's BF16 checkpoint is 51.6 GB](https://huggingface.co/surogate/rune-26b-a4b-GGUF/raw/main/README.md), making it a poor local training starting point. [INFERENCE] No custom attention/MoE architecture until cheap training and inference baselines establish an actual bottleneck.
