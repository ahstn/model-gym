# Visual-quality pilot: research and training preflight

Checked: 2026-09-29. Scope: techniques, objectives, source coverage, teacher labels, and evaluation before the first image pilot. The methods workstream used an agent with ultra reasoning. Exa supplied discovery and primary-source reads across four workstreams. The [search and local-evidence ledger](exa-results/pilot-preflight-2026-09-29.json) records queries, URLs, fetch status, and manifest observations.

**The next step is label and split preparation. Supervised pilot training should wait.** The research already covers most major method families. The useful changes are a compact-student experiment, stronger evaluation of image selection and local defects, and several concrete corrections. No evidence found makes another large image download a prerequisite. This is a broad gap audit, not proof that every relevant paper has been found.

## 1. What the actual pilot can support

The [assembly record](../datasets/pilot-10k-assembly.md) remains valid: 10,000 training records, 400 development records, and 400 test records were assembled and verified. Structural verification does not establish representative splits, valid training targets, or model accuracy.

Read-only checks on 2026-09-29 found:

| Check | Observed state | Required decision |
|---|---|---|
| IQA and aesthetics supervision | All 10,800 quality targets and all 10,800 aesthetics targets are null; their task masks are off. VizWiz supplies defect votes, not MOS. | Create a separate versioned label artifact. The assembly verifier intentionally rejects non-null ratings; do not overwrite the frozen source manifests. |
| Photo-domain supervision | 1,200 negative labels, zero positive labels, and 9,600 unknowns. | Establish reviewed positives, including badly damaged photos. A negative-only domain target cannot train a useful eligibility classifier. |
| Test allocation | One 83-row duplicate-candidate group contains 81 of the 100 VizWiz test photos and two Megalith photos. VizWiz test has only 16 candidate groups. | Review this component before freezing model evaluation. A perceptual-hash group is not verified shared-scene content. Do not split confirmed duplicates just to meet quotas. |
| Authentic-fault distribution | At least three unrecognizable votes occur in 206/1,000 VizWiz train images, 23/100 development images, and 85/100 test images. | The test overrepresents extreme capture failures relative to train/development. Retain it as a named stress slice or create a versioned, reviewed replacement allocation before model selection. |
| Distortion evaluation | All 3,000 generated photo variants are in train; neither holdout contains such variants. | Create a separate diagnostic manifest from held-out parents. Keep it distinct from the original 400-row holdouts. |
| Delivered view | 936/1,000 distortion parents were resized, affecting 2,808 variants. All variants have a maximum edge at most 1,536 pixels; 5,037/5,400 Megalith originals exceed that size. | Compare parents and variants through the same viewing policy. Original-versus-variant differences otherwise include resizing as well as the intended operator. |
| Controls | Owned controls include damaged examples, but share a renderer, dimensions, background style, and visible type titles. Each holdout contains one owned template per kind. | Test unseen control styles and difficult photo/non-photo boundaries. Source or renderer recognition is not proof of a general domain rule. |
| External overlap | No external exclusion manifest was supplied. Byte/pixel checks and local grouping passed. | Clear overlap with the named external tests used in a claim. Otherwise report internal feasibility only; local checks do not establish absence of backbone-pretraining contamination. |

The large test component contains 40 distinct dHash values; 39 records have an all-zero dHash. It contains 2,232 of the 2,237 saved near-match edges. The builder joins close hashes transitively and allocates large groups to test first. This explains the allocation mechanism; it does not prove that the photos are duplicates. Low-detail images can require a different duplicate-review rule. Review image content and provenance before deciding which edges to keep. Preserve the original manifests and publish any replacement under a new version.

The source of these observations is the local manifests plus [family grouping](../../src/visual_quality/validation.py) and [split allocation](../../src/visual_quality/assemble.py). Counts describe candidate groups, not proven independent scenes. No images, splits, or source caches were changed during this audit.

## 2. Methods: what to use now and what to defer

“Use now” below means include in the next experiment design after its data gates pass. Published model results are not measurements on our pilot or hardware.

| Technique | Coverage and primary evidence | Decision for this pilot |
|---|---|---|
| Compact pointwise and pairwise distillation | **Already covered, higher priority.** [LEAF](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.20689) uses a frozen teacher and a ConvNeXt scalar student. The point target is the expected quality-word score with SmoothL1 loss; pairs add a separate ranking loss. A 28.6M Tiny student is close to the 88.6M Base in the reported AGIQA-3K experiment with 10% human MOS. | Start with a small scalar head and point-only supervision. Add pairs as a separate controlled change. API scalar targets are an adaptation, not an exact reproduction of LEAF's token-logit targets. |
| Frozen quality features versus limited adaptation | **Already covered, missing controlled run.** ARNIQA, Re-IQA, CONTRIQUE, and [SigLIP2+AGM](https://github.com/drkkgy/NR_IQA_AGM) are in the inventory. A [SigLIP LoRA study](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.02002v2) also supports checking adaptation under source-disjoint evaluation. | Compare a cached-feature scalar head with limited adaptation of the same encoder, labels, and views. Count full serving cost, not only trainable parameters. Check the chosen checkpoint's terms and prior training sources. |
| Direct ranking without human MOS | **New candidate.** [HiRQA v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.15130v1) learns a scorer from synthetic ordering, image-text alignment, and embedding consistency. HiRQA-S uses ResNet-18. Its training uses 140K pristine sources and about 700K variants per epoch; it is not a 10K result. The [release](https://github.com/uf-robopi/HiRQA) did not establish a license in the fetched material. | Add a small research baseline if release terms permit. Test reviewed same-parent, same-operator ranks as an auxiliary arm. Do not infer absolute MOS or cross-operator ordering from severity indices. |
| Reasoning distilled into an encoder | **Already covered, higher priority.** [RALI v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.11369v2) scores without an LLM at inference after learning from quality reasoning. | Include a compact released assessor in the efficiency screen. Copy the reasoning-distillation method only if simpler scalar/pair training leaves a measured gap. |
| Local and mixed distortions | **Partly covered.** SHDIQA and native crops are already listed. [SLIDE-IQA](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29162) adds evidence that global-distortion pretraining can miss spatially bounded and co-occurring defects. | Add local-versus-global diagnostic cases now. Later, compare a small amount of realistic local/mixed damage with the same count of global variants. The entire dual-encoder method is not required for the first run. |
| Structured self-supervised features | **New follow-up.** [SHAMISA](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13669) uses compositional distortions and relation graphs. Its frozen encoder still needs a human-MOS ridge readout and uses several crops/scales. | Defer unless simpler features fail. “Label-free pretraining” does not mean that the final score needs no human labels. |
| Ordered objectives | **Partly covered.** NIMA/EMD, DeQA, and regression heads are already reviewed. [ORCU v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.15658v3) and [D3O](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.23575) add ordinal calibration and distribution objectives. | After scalar targets are validated, compare scalar, hard-bin, and ordered-distribution heads. Model probabilities and API confidence must not be described as human rating histograms. Do not force a unimodal target onto genuine split human opinions without testing it. |
| Multi-crop teacher, global student | **New follow-up.** [Patch Knowledge Transfer](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05605) distills local/global information on supervised AIGC IQA tasks. | Defer until a crop ablation gives a measured benefit. Distillation cannot be assumed to recover defects absent from the student's input pixels. |
| Human review selected by uncertainty or disagreement | **Useful extension.** [LBPS-EIC](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.18372v2) studies uncertainty-based selection of pair comparisons. [Multimodal Bayesian prompt ensembles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08777) study judge calibration on generated-image preference tasks. | Start with a stratified random human audit. Add a bounded disagreement sample later, with a random control and explicit inclusion reasons. Keep the final human gold independent of model-predicted preferences. These papers do not validate verbal confidence from our teachers. |
| Label-efficient coreset selection | **Do not adopt its headline saving.** [WCPC](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026W/DataCV/papers/Shinde_When_Every_Label_Counts_Wasserstein-Optimal_Dataset_Representation_for_Coreset_Selection_CVPRW_2026_paper.pdf) reports an upper-bound experiment with oracle access to full-pool score statistics. Proxy MOS and pilot annotations are proposed substitutes. | It is not demonstrated as a drop-in selector for our unlabeled pool. First compare random stratification with simple content coverage; do not claim the paper's annotation saving. |

RL, large VLM grids, and long generated explanations remain later options. Existing evidence does not make them prerequisites for a low-cost image scorer. A single small experiment should change one objective, input policy, or data component at a time.

### Calibration and score semantics

LEAF's calibration fine-tuning changes the student using MSE plus a correlation loss on human MOS. It is different from fitting a post-hoc scale transform. A strictly increasing transform of a fixed prediction preserves its rank order. The earlier mixture notes now distinguish direct teacher predictions, distilled-student results, and further student training.

Keep technical quality, aesthetics, recognizability, and photo-domain eligibility separate. VizWiz defect votes are auxiliary labels. A clean chart can have high technical quality and aesthetics while remaining excluded by the photo policy. Known generated origin is provenance; it is not always recoverable from pixels.

The [prompt pack](synthetic-label-prompts.md) is the effective teacher contract: scalar ratings are primary; words and elicited weights are alternatives. Its bin boundaries now match the pinned Q-ReAlign mapper: 1.8 enters poor, 2.6 fair, 3.4 good, and 4.2 excellent. Keep the raw scalar and version the conversion. No label importer or trainer was added in this audit.

## 4. Minimum first-run plan

These are project proposals, not claims that the cited papers validated this exact recipe.

1. **Resolve the split decision before model selection.** Review the concentrated test component using pixels and provenance. Preserve confirmed families. Record any corrected grouping and source/defect balance in a new manifest version, or label the existing concentration as a stress test and supply a separate representative holdout. Freeze the result before seeing candidate-model scores.
2. **Define gold and labeled artifacts.** Start with a small blind development annotation trial, then audit the current development cohort or a declared representative subset. Prefer multiple independent ratings per image and record repeats, disagreements, ties, and recognizability. Open human-rated anchors can support the screen, but their domain and terms must match the claim. Separate human data used for student calibration training from teacher/model selection and final evaluation. The old proposed 1K teacher screen is not the existing 400-row development manifest.
3. **Freeze the view and label contract.** Record delivered-pixel hashes, dimensions, orientation, resampling, and crop coordinates. State whether the target describes the delivered view or the original image. Give parents and variants the same spatial processing. Keep quality and aesthetics losses masked unless their own targets pass review; collect both domain positives and negatives. Store accepted labels, rejected attempts, source/prompt/model versions, and split links in a new artifact that refers to the immutable source IDs.
4. **Freeze a small diagnostic sidecar and decision rule.** Use unseen development parents for operator checks, local-versus-global defects, and subtle/severe examples. Include ties and intentional photographic effects. Define primary endpoints, acceptable regression margins, minimum useful gains, and comparison baselines before scoring. If external test overlap cannot be cleared, limit conclusions to internal feasibility.
5. **Screen frozen anchors cheaply.** Compare Q-Align with one compact released assessor on the same image-only slice and hardware. Include a small specialist such as RALI or an existing quality encoder after its checkpoint/source terms are checked. Measure batch-1 latency, batch throughput, peak memory, failures, and quality together. Do not turn paper throughput or trainable-parameter counts into local cost estimates.
6. **Train one simple student, then one change.** Use a cached single-encoder scalar head with accepted pointwise targets. Next compare limited adaptation of the same encoder. Add reviewed within-parent ranks or named-defect supervision as one separate ablation. Cap or weight repeated parent/template families. Keep teacher judgment, human MOS, and transformation metadata in distinct fields and losses.
7. **Promote only on independent task evidence.** Test a small VLM, soft/ordinal heads, native crops, or additional data only when the simpler run exposes a specific gap. Use human agreement for IQA and aesthetics separately; same-teacher imitation is not human accuracy. An image-only pilot makes no VQA claim. Keep the human-rated research-mix baseline and larger backbone grid as separate later experiments.

The training start gate is explicit: accepted labels for each enabled target, both classes for a learned domain gate, a documented split decision, fixed input views, and a frozen evaluation plan. A one-seed feasibility result can validate the pipeline; superiority still needs locked tests, source-aware uncertainty estimates, and confirmation across seeds.

## 5. Report the right endpoints

| Target | Required pilot evidence |
|---|---|
| Technical quality | Per-source and per-dataset SRCC, raw PLCC, KRCC and sample/family counts where human ratings exist; same-parent pair accuracy by distortion; failures and missing predictions. |
| Aesthetics | Independent human agreement, matched-subject pair/selection accuracy, and score spread. Report technical/aesthetic disagreement cases rather than combining the targets. |
| Photo eligibility | Confusion counts, false acceptance of non-photos, and false rejection of photos at a development-chosen threshold. Use AUROC/PR-AUC only with a score, and probability calibration metrics only with probability outputs. |
| Selection utility | At fixed selection size: human preference or regret, unacceptable-image rate, duplicate-family concentration, and scene coverage. These are proposed product diagnostics, not new MOS benchmarks. |
| Generalization | Within-source and within-quality-band results, unseen parents/control styles, and external source overlap status. Include a source-only or simple resolution/sharpness predictor as a shortcut diagnostic. |
| Efficiency | Same hardware and delivered views; include decode/preprocessing, batch-1 p50/p95, throughput, memory, invalid outputs, and cost per accepted prediction. |

Compute correlation within each human study. Combine paired metric differences only with fixed weights within the same task; never concatenate incompatible MOS scales. Resample independent source families inside each dataset. Specify a non-inferiority margin before testing; overlapping confidence intervals alone do not show equivalence. Small subgroups and uncertain duplicate families may make a result inconclusive.

## 6. Coverage and limits

The four tracks covered methods/objectives, datasets/sampling, evaluations, and teacher-label/viewing contracts. They ran 20 Exa searches requesting 180 result slots. The 180 returned entries reduce to 164 exact unique URLs; 48 unique source URLs were successfully fetched. Search hits include secondary pages and repeated publications, and different URLs can describe the same paper. The ledger distinguishes discovery from fetched evidence; a hit was not treated as validated merely because it ranked highly. We followed promising leads into primary papers, release cards, and pinned code, then checked the conclusions against local manifests.

The audit does not re-rank every current API teacher, measure new label accuracy, reproduce published model results, settle every dataset's terms, or certify absence of pretraining contamination. No scoring, training, bulk acquisition, or dataset upload occurred. The source inventory is adequate for the next bounded preparation step; labels, split review, and evaluation design are the current constraints.
