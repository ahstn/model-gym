# Expanded eligibility optimization: 120-view panels

[The report](report.md) compares ten additional Liquid d1.1 and Jev 1.14 eligibility questions against the original and each model's previous revision. [The interactive comparison](gallery/expanded-eligibility.html) shows the original, prior revision and selected prompt for 240 views. The selected prompts are experimental and are not deployed automatically.

The [protocol](protocol.md), [reference manifest](reference-manifest.json), [predeclared variants](variants.json), [original prior revisions](previous-prompts.json), and [selection rule](selection-rule.json) were fixed before inspecting results. [winner.json](winner.json) contains every development round's aggregate and paired metrics, and [results.json](results.json) contains the held-out case-level results and comparisons. [boundary-sensitivity.json](boundary-sensitivity.json) records three questionable historical labels before holdout responses were seen; the report shows both the frozen and alternative interpretations.

The original holdout coordinator scheduled no requests because it used the stage name as the partition name. The [driver amendment](holdout-driver-amendment.json) documents the corrected filter, without changing the frozen prompt selection. The original empty stage and corrected stage have separate logs.

The panels are balanced and family-disjoint, with 120 views each. The 10 historical flat works in each panel were scored previously; development includes known prior failures, while held-out historical works were selected using prior reference judgments. Four public-domain Met works were obtained from its Open Access API; further acquisition was prevented by access errors documented in [acquisition-diagnostic.json](acquisition-diagnostic.json). Each panel also contains 28 synthetic screenshots generated from source photos in the same panel, 10 synthetic charts and 10 rendered scenes. Thus neither all 240 views nor their classes are independent, randomly sampled, or representative of deployment. Human review is still needed. A similarity audit of source photographs is in [cross-split-similarity-audit.json](cross-split-similarity-audit.json).

Per-attempt request/response evidence is archived in `execution-records/` after both jobs stop, including failures. Scores and precise prompt/view hashes are retained; parsed JSON and a wire-text hash are saved, but the exact provider wire text is not retained. Images used for requests reside locally under the `rehearsal-4000-2026-11-01`, `eligibility-optimization-2026-11-01`, and `eligibility-expanded-120-2026-11-01` data directories, rather than in the Git archive. The result and prompt files therefore do not reproduce image access on other hosts without those assets. Preprocessing copies that enlarge a short image side are identified in the manifest.

`prepare_candidates.py` and `acquire_met.py` refuse to overwrite acquired evidence. `build_manifest.py` reconstructs into a separate `reference-manifest-rebuilt.json`, not the frozen manifest. Regenerating calls requires provider access and incurs cost. Exact absolute source paths and timestamped hashes are retained to describe the original run, not as a portable automation interface. Operational logs, verification outputs and tests are stored alongside this file.
