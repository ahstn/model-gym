# Expanded eligibility prompt trial

Test ten predeclared additional eligibility-question variants, r11–r20, on 120 development views per model. Compare both the original decision question and each model's previously selected revision (Liquid r03, Jev r06). Keep the same routes, common instructions, structured three-question decision schema, native quality questions and 0.50 exclusion threshold. Select only on development; freeze one candidate per model before running the 120-image holdout. Holdout runs original, previous revision and selected candidate on each model.

Each panel contains 60 eligible photos and 60 excluded views: 30 photos of physical objects/printed contexts, 30 other scenes, 10 flat historical works already model-scored in the 4,000 corpus, 2 newly sourced public-domain museum works, 10 charts, 10 synthetic renders and 28 synthetic interface views. The screenshot photos are paired in their own split; the development and holdout family sets are disjoint. The ten development historical works were scored in the earlier eligibility trial and include known failures; the ten held-out historical works were scored in the 4,000 run, but not used in the prior eligibility trial. The remaining sampled source families were absent from that run and the preceding eligibility panels. Some near-duplicates could remain. Four synthetic UI layouts are split by panel; the screenshot controls are not representative of the variety of real interfaces. Reference labels were assistant visual judgments, not human ground truth. Both classes are deliberately balanced and not deployment-prevalence estimates.

On each model's paired development results, require at least 114/120 valid and at least 57/60 valid in each class. To replace its prior revision, a new prompt must not increase false floors on cases paired with the prior revision, and must reduce total paired errors. Among eligible variants choose lowest total errors, then fewest false floors, then fewest missed exclusions, then the shortest instruction, then round ID. If no candidate qualifies, retain the prior revision. This rule makes no claim of production safety and doesn't optimize on the holdout. Recompute category-specific and paired performance; record missing results, latency, price and native quality score drift without claiming quality-accuracy change. Never substitute labels or adjust the threshold.

Every request and response is retained in a separate data directory. Existing runner safeguards: up to three attempts only for verified image-processing 503s; do not replay successful, invalid, or interrupted results; per-model $10 conservative cap including reservations for unpriced attempts; stop on authorization or unforeseen errors. API workers are bounded. Preserve raw parsed provider JSON and response-text hash; wire text is not retained. All input manifests and prompt variants are hash-frozen before calls.
