PHOTOGRAPHY ELIGIBILITY RULE — APPLY BEFORE EITHER QUALITY RUBRIC

This task selects photographic images. First decide whether the delivered image is visibly a photograph of a physical scene or object, or clearly excluded non-photographic content. This eligibility rule takes precedence over all quality anchors and scoring clarifications below.

Excluded content includes screenshots with visible software/browser/game interface; flat poster, advertisement, slide, document, diagram, infographic or page reproductions; paintings and illustrations presented as the image itself; and clearly synthetic graphics or obvious computer-rendered scenes. A poster or artwork reproduction remains excluded even if its typography, composition or rendering is attractive. A nearly full-frame reproduction of a printed page or artwork is excluded when the reproduction itself is the content, with no meaningful photographed scene around it.

For clearly excluded content, return BOTH technical_quality and aesthetics with status "ok", score "1.00", and limitations ["unsupported_domain"]. Each evidence sentence must state the visible content type and that the photography eligibility rule causes the floor. Do not separately reward its graphic design, artistic merit, legibility or clean rendering. This floor means unsuitable for the photographic dataset, not a claim that the original artwork is intrinsically poor.

Ordinary photographs can contain text, signs, labels, screens, posters, artwork, sculptures, carvings or manufactured objects. Keep them eligible when visible perspective, depth, lighting, surroundings or physical context establish a photographed scene or object. For example, a bottle label on a bottle, a sign in a street, an artwork in a gallery scene, or a stone carving is not excluded merely because of its subject. A framed artwork photographed only as a flat full-frame reproduction is different from a gallery scene with meaningful surrounding context.

Do not infer a screenshot, AI origin or computer generation from low resolution, compression, blur, noise, vivid color, an unusual subject or imperfect anatomy alone. If a realistic image has no clear visual evidence of an excluded content type, assess it as photographic; do not claim to authenticate its origin. A photograph presented without visible interface cannot be identified as a screenshot solely from the pixels. Ambiguous style can be noted using "ambiguous_style" without applying the floor.

For eligible photographs, score technical quality and aesthetics independently using the existing rubrics and clarifications. Do not lower either score merely because the other is low. Do not emit a third score or any extra JSON keys. A missing or unreadable image still uses the existing unscorable/null behavior, not the non-photographic floor.
