# Eligibility prompt optimisation protocol

Compare the exact previous Liquid d1.1 and Jev 1.14 Decisions routes, common state, three questions, quality anchors, normalization and 0.50 eligibility threshold. Change only the exclusion question's instructions; the original question is the paired baseline. No new image quality model, implicit threshold adjustment, substitute provider, or label overwrite is permitted.

The fresh reference set contains 80 views: 40 development and 40 held out, each balanced 20 eligible / 20 excluded. It uses assistant-reviewed, previously unscored source families from the frozen candidate pool, three public-domain Commons works, and 19 explicitly synthetic browser-interface screenshots paired with fresh photos in the same partition. It is deliberately enriched for domain-boundary examples, not representative of deployment prevalence. Five natural flat artwork/document examples, simple chart/render controls, and synthetic screenshots do not fully characterize real-world posters and screenshots. A separate 20-image, balanced natural challenge was already scored in the previous 4,000-image trial and includes two known prior disagreements; it is diagnostic, not fresh or unbiased. Source/encoding and reference judgments are recorded in reference-manifest.json. Physical cases were reviewed as individual cells in contact sheets before model responses; judgments are assistant opinions, not human ratings.

Run 10 distinct prompt revisions in numbered order. Rounds 1–5 are declared before the first API call. Review development results only; document how rounds 6–10 are chosen before calling them. Retain all failures and unpriced attempts. Original baseline and each candidate use both unchanged quality questions as well as the exclusion question. Only the 40 development images are sent during prompt selection. Freeze the winning prompt per model before sending any held-out or historical challenge image for baseline or winner scoring.

Select each model's winner on the development set without reference to held-out results: at least 95% overall and 19/20 usable outputs in each class; false exclusions no greater than the baseline count plus one; minimize the sum of false exclusions and missed exclusions, then false exclusions, then prompt length, then round number. A candidate must reduce missed exclusions versus the baseline and must not increase total errors; otherwise retain the baseline and describe candidates as exploratory. The one-case tolerance is 5 percentage points, not a claim of proven noninferiority.

Report class denominators, missed exclusions, false floors and coverage for both models, with paired comparisons and uncertainty appropriate to small counts. Measure technical and aesthetic native-score shifts on eligible cases versus baseline; without independent quality ratings these are drift checks, not quality-accuracy estimates. Never infer correctness from the models' own confidence, nor tune thresholds or prompts to the held-out/historical results. Retain the original three task outputs, per-request latency, all provider costs and unknown receipts, plus exact prompt, source/view and response hashes. At most three attempts per job for verified image-processing 503s; no replay of successful or invalid responses. Unknown charges retain a conservative per-route reservation and per-model $10 ceiling. The trial is exploratory and requires a wider human-reviewed fresh benchmark before deployment.
