# Eligibility prompt optimisation, 2026-11-01

This is a 10-round evaluation of clearer photo eligibility wording for Liquid d1.1 and Jev 1.14. [Report and results](report.md) describe the selection, scores, failure cases, latency, costs and limitations; [the interactive gallery](gallery/eligibility-trial.html) presents all trial views and the post-hoc size diagnostic. The live local/LAN gallery serves those same files under `/gallery/eligibility-trial.html`.

The original common instructions, structured three-question decision schema, quality questions and 0.50 eligibility cutoff remain unchanged. Candidate wording appears in [rounds-first.json](rounds-first.json) and [rounds-second.json](rounds-second.json). The prompts were selected using development only, with the choices and timestamps recorded in [winners.json](winners.json) and [winners-freeze.json](winners-freeze.json). Neither revision is deployed automatically or substituted into the original trial.

The [reference manifest](reference-manifest.json) includes 40 fresh development and 40 fresh held-out views, each half eligible photographs and half excluded content, plus 20 previously model-scored natural challenge views. All eligibility labels are assistant assessments, not human ground truth. Screenshots are synthetic controls of a shared UI design; only two fresh held-out images are natural flat artwork or documents. These are intentionally balanced, family-split diagnostic cases, not a representative random sample. The Jev results for original held-out W0002 are missing; [resolution-retest-results.json](resolution-retest-results.json) records the separate, resized-copy diagnostic.

[candidate-selection-fresh.json](candidate-selection-fresh.json) and `review/fresh-*` record the candidates used for the fresh set. `candidate-selection.json` and `review/candidates-*` are earlier, superseded exploratory materials involving previously scored candidates; `poster-candidates.json` and `review/poster-*` are additional embedding-based exploration. The three `review/commons-*.json` files contain source metadata for the selected public-domain works. Further Commons acquisition hit a 429 rate limit. `prepare_set.py` reconstructs only the initial 80 fresh cases to a new `reference-manifest-initial.json`; it does not regenerate or overwrite the frozen 100-case manifest or historical challenge.

Per-attempt request and parsed-response records are archived in [execution-records](execution-records/index.json) after all workers stopped, with hashes and credential checks. Raw image views are stored separately under `/home/ahstn/git/model-gym/visual-quality/data/eligibility-optimization-2026-11-01/views/` and the previous rehearsal data directory; those local paths are not part of the Git archive. The report and results are not self-contained without the local image assets. Provider JSON is saved as parsed data, with a hash of the original wire text; the raw wire text itself was not retained.

[verification.json](verification.json) records freeze checks, data joins and recounts; [gallery/validation.json](gallery/validation.json) records browser checks. Operational logs are in `operations/`. The included tests cover the runner and analysis safeguards. Recreating a trial would require the original image files, provider access and any needed API keys; replaying inference may incur cost and need not reproduce stochastic outputs.
