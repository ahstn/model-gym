Original and selected eligibility questions use the same image, quality anchors and 0.50 exclusion threshold. An excluded decision floors both scores to 1.00. Reference eligibility is an assistant judgment, not a human label. Fresh holdout contains 20 eligible photos and 20 excluded views; synthetic browser screenshots are paired with eligible photos and are not independent families. The natural challenge was previously model-scored and includes two known disagreements. Development images were used to choose prompts. The separate resolution diagnostic is a resized copy of W0002, shown only as a post-hoc check.

Scores show two decimals; exclusion outputs show four so values close to the 0.50 threshold remain distinguishable. Native scores before a floor, request latency and prices are available in the saved results.

These are selected diagnostic cases, not an estimate of deployment prevalence. Inspect exact views before interpreting a disagreement.
