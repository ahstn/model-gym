# Matched 50-image trial with separate technical and aesthetic ratings

The user authorized 50 fresh images, each assessed jointly by RunInfra `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017`, OpenRouter `google/gemini-3.8-flash` at medium reasoning, and OpenRouter `openai/gpt-6-luna` at high reasoning. The question is which student best matches independent assistant image judgments on each construct. Do not combine technical quality and aesthetics into one quality score or claim human accuracy.

Selection uses seed 20261027 and preserves the historical mixture: 24 controlled images (six complete four-image families, two families per blur/noise/JPEG operator), 14 VizWiz, and 12 standalone Megalith, totaling 32 families. Exclude all prior exposed families including the completed 200-image trial, exact hashes and conservative near duplicates. All source rows remain in the training partition. Full-frame RGB/sRGB PNGs are capped at edge 1536 without enlargement, then reduced only when necessary to fit 2,650,000 bytes. Each controlled family shares one edge cap. Reviewers and all three routes receive identical hashed PNG bytes. Results describe these supplied views, not unseen original detail.

Use the existing visual-rating-v1 system and joint rubrics unchanged. The output has exactly two keys, technical_quality and aesthetics, each with task, status, score, evidence and limitations. Score transport is the previous exact string enum from "1.00" to "5.00" in "0.10" steps, plus null for unscorable. This limits strings to two decimal places without implying hundredth-point judgment precision. A named Decimal adapter converts only valid strings into numeric observations; no rounding or repair. The sole additional output wording targets 12–20 evidence words while retaining the existing hard 30-word limit, responding to the earlier run's overlong explanations. The same schema and wording apply to all three routes. Strict schema enforcement cannot establish the truth of an explanation.
