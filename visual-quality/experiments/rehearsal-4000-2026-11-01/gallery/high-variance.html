The default ranking is the average of the population variances of the technical and aesthetic baseline scores across five independent models (Luna, DeepSeek, Liquid, Jev and Q-ReAlign Lite). Both scores are on the same 1–5 scale. Higher means more disagreement, not necessarily an error. The reference and derived mean are shown for context and excluded from ranking. Every selected model or treatment arm must have both scores; ties are ordered by image ID. Lite has no photography-scope gate and can score graphics highly, so it can dominate disagreement.
