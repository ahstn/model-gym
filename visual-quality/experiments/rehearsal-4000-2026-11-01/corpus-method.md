# Rehearsal corpus and content sampling

The requested corpus contains 4,000 delivered images from 3,700 recorded source families. The 500-image reference benchmark contains distinct, previously unscored source families. EmbeddingGemma 2 is used for content coverage, related-image discovery and possible duplicate review; neither embedding similarity nor cluster membership supplies a quality label.

Family counts refer to the recorded source identifiers. Visually suspected capture-series groups are tracked separately as `reviewed_related_group`; they are not silently merged or described as proven exact duplicates. When a member enters the benchmark, its reviewed partner is excluded from background selection, percentile calibration and stats donors.

## Source frame and preparation

Use the acquired `pilot-10k-v1` training partition only. Preserve its existing development/test partitions, excluding family, source-group, byte/pixel hash matches and conservative dHash distance-at-most-two matches. The original 10,000 training rows represent 6,850 recorded families. Original source-label and caption fields never enter content embeddings, selection scores or grader prompts. Human VizWiz defect votes are used solely to describe/check capture-defect coverage.

All sources pass the same frozen view renderer used in the earlier scoring trials: EXIF transpose, embedded ICC conversion to sRGB, alpha compositing on white, full-frame LANCZOS and RGB PNG output. Start at a maximum edge of 1536; rerender the original bytes at successively smaller 0.9 size caps when the PNG exceeds 2,650,000 bytes. All models receive the identical frozen PNG. Controlled parents and their three variants share one size cap. Common native-model limits also require minimum edge 256 and area 78,400; no images are upscaled, padded or cropped to meet those limits.

Preparation found 6,081 valid unique representative views: 4,289 Megalith, 992 VizWiz and 800 CLEVR/PlotQA controls. It rejected 708 multi-frame/MPO files, three ICC conversion failures and eight images outside the shared dimension/area limits. Multi-frame files are not silently reduced to the first frame. These exclusions limit the sampling frame and should not be mistaken for image-quality judgments.

The frozen candidate pool has 5,500 representatives: 4,289 Megalith, 992 VizWiz and 219 controls. The requested 4500/800/200 allocation was adjusted after decoding exclusions, before embeddings or grader calls. The benchmark candidate prefix has 1,000 fresh standalone families: 800 Megalith, 160 VizWiz and 40 controls. Historical exposure comes from the prior frozen exclusion audit plus subsequent experiment manifests. Fresh means absent from these recorded scoring/review trials; it makes no claim about model pretraining.

## Embeddings and content groups

Use actual `google/embeddinggemma-2` image embeddings, revision `f9972ac22e696a105c482202646979e33e4b5a1e`, with audio disabled, CPU FP32, 768 dimensions, L2 normalization and 280 image soft tokens. Two workers use six CPU threads each and batch four, prioritizing the first 1,000 candidates so independent reference review can begin while the remaining embeddings run. Identical previously embedded view bytes may reuse a vector only when the checkpoint and all inference software versions match; every reuse is recorded.

The inference environment is the prior isolated cache: torch 2.14.0+cpu, transformers 5.22.1, sentence-transformers 6.2.0, numpy 2.5.4 and Pillow 13.0.0. The delivered-view and image-stats stages retain the original view environment with Pillow 12.3.0 and numpy 2.5.3. Embedding preprocessing acts on already-frozen PNGs; it does not change the grader inputs.

Fit 16 content clusters for benchmark selection and 40 for the full candidate pool, using KMeans with 20 initializations and experiment seed 202611014000 (KMeans uses its uint32 reduction, 747551088). Names are later manual descriptions, not classifier labels. Candidate-family representatives, rather than controlled variants, determine content-group sizes. For the rehearsal, capacity-capped square-root cluster allocation and farthest-first selection within source/operator quotas reduce dominance by large groups while retaining explicit capture-defect and nonphotographic controls. Similarity thresholds identify review candidates; they are not proof of duplicate images.

## Benchmark and rehearsal

The benchmark has 100 development, 300 representative evaluation and 100 challenge images. Development and representative source quotas are 80/16/4 and 240/48/12 for Megalith/VizWiz/controls. Within these source categories, proportional cluster allocation and seeded random selection preserve the declared 80%/16%/4% proportions of the guarded 1,000-candidate benchmark frame. These proportions are not exact population weights for the final 4,000 images. The 100 challenge cases cover low sharpness, sharp noise, darkness, clipping/brightness, low contrast, capture context, high detail, content diversity and ten explicit nonphotographic source controls. Six of the ten content-diversity slots are reserved for visually reviewed full-frame reproductions and photographs containing physical artwork or screens; EmbeddingGemma text/image retrieval surfaced these candidates before reference scoring. The retrieval judgments are not supplied to blind reference reviewers. Challenge statistics are reported separately from representative evaluation.

No retained benchmark candidate pair may have cosine similarity at or above 0.985, a conservative pairwise diversity constraint. This may exclude related-but-distinct scenes and does not assert that all flagged neighbours are duplicates. Contact sheets and pair similarities remain available for review. The early benchmark retains fixed case IDs, view hashes, family IDs and partitions when incorporated into the full corpus. The sole predeclared exception is objectively confirmed historical exposure or duplicate-family leakage discovered during the later full-pool review. Such a case is replaced before any benchmark model calls, with superseded review identities and blinded review provenance retained.

The full 4,000 target mix is 3,000 Megalith images including 100 controlled parents, 600 VizWiz, 300 controlled derivatives and 100 nonphotographic controls. Controlled families contain one original plus three severities: 34 blur, 33 noise and 33 JPEG families. They are a separate paired diagnostic, outside the 500 independent-family benchmark. Generation records and actual delivered pixels validate the stimuli; a flat sharpness/noise/blockiness proxy alone is not grounds for deleting a family.

For those controlled families, reconstruct the original generation's canonical 1,536-edge parent and verify its preprocessing metadata against every recorded variant. All four images then pass through the same final byte-budget cap. This avoids comparing a directly resized original with a twice-resized derivative. Record the canonical parent hash, final parent hash, original source hash, transform lineage and shared final dimensions. Each family must contain four distinct decoded pixel arrays. Content embeddings remain attached to the original candidate parent view, even when the family's final cap differs; the related-image export marks this basis and inheritance for derivatives.

## Statistics and swapped evidence

Image-stats runs on the exact grader views at max_edge=1536. Metadata and source blockiness are recorded from the original files separately, with source hashes. The reference is calibrated on 3,200 nonbenchmark family representatives: all 500 benchmark families and all 300 controlled derivatives are excluded. No reference scores or grader outputs are used for percentile calibration.

`focus_area` remains in raw metrics but is omitted from prompt text. The text warns that measurements can reflect scene content and deliberate effects, and confines their intended use to technical-quality checking. All model outputs and raw scores are retained; gate flags only prioritize review and never skip a call or replace a score in this trial.

For the counterfactual arm, each benchmark image receives another image's stats from a unique nonbenchmark family representative in the same 4,000 corpus. Match source category, original encoding and approximate delivered dimensions before choosing contradictory sharpness directions. Record all metric deltas, matching relaxations and actual direction balance. This is not a 500-image permutation: donors come from the background corpus, without donor labels entering the prompt.

All task-training masks remain false. The corpus, labels and assistant references are experimental evidence, not approved training data.
