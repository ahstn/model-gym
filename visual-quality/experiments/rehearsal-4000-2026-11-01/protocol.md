# Four-thousand-image rehearsal and fresh benchmark

Authorized 1 November 2026. Status: preparation. This protocol is written before selection is finalized, reference review, and new scoring. Changes required by preparation will be recorded before inference rather than silently replacing a frozen run.

## Scope

Build 4,000 delivered image views using real EmbeddingGemma 2 image embeddings to improve subject coverage and surface related images for duplicate review. Preserve explicit source and defect quotas. The planned mix is 3,000 Megalith images, 600 VizWiz photographs, 300 controlled derivatives from 100 of the Megalith parents, and 100 non-photographic controls: 3,700 recorded source families before any additional duplicate grouping. Embedding similarity is not a quality label or conclusive evidence of duplication.

Select 500 previously unscored/unreviewed source families for independent assistant reference review: 100 development, 300 representative evaluation and 100 challenge cases. These are a nested subset of the 4,000 views, not additional images. The representative panel samples the declared noncontrolled family frame; controlled ladders are a separate diagnostic. Historical reference and known scored-family exclusions, original protected splits, perceptual duplicate candidates and final family identities are audited by the preparation stage. No training-approval mask is enabled.

All five baseline scorers assess all 4,000 images: GPT-6 Luna with high reasoning through OpenRouter, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 with high reasoning through RunInfra, Liquid d1.1 and Jev 1.14 through the prior OpenRouter Decisions interface, and Q-ReAlign Lite 4B through Runpod. Haiku and Gemini are outside this run. Retain exact existing baseline prompts and scoring contracts. Record requested reasoning separately from actual returned usage/evidence. Do not silently substitute a model or provider.

The 500 reference cases additionally receive two Luna and DeepSeek treatments: own-image stats and another image's stats. There are 20,000 baseline image/model results and 2,000 additional image/model/treatment results, plus 500 independent assistant reviews. Each result contains separate technical and aesthetic scores. The Luna/DeepSeek mean is a derived comparison, not an additional request or independent reference.

## Images, clustering and statistical references

Use full-frame images under the existing maximum-1536-pixel delivery policy with source/view hashes. As in the prior preparation, reduce the whole frame in documented steps when needed to satisfy the shared 2.65 MB image budget; keep controlled families at a common delivery size. Record actual dimensions and excluded source-decode failures. All scorers receive identical delivered bytes. Fit content clusters on one representative per source family; controlled derivatives do not increase a family's clustering weight. Preserve source, capture-defect and scope-control coverage when sampling across clusters. Retain candidate-pool counts, cluster sizes, selection probabilities where available, reviewed duplicate candidates and final sampling decisions. Near-duplicate families cannot straddle benchmark partitions.

Compute image-stats on the exact delivered views. Original-file metadata and source blockiness remain distinct from rendered-view measurements and carry original hashes. Build and freeze the percentile reference from nonbenchmark background family representatives, excluding all benchmark families. Keep algorithm, preprocessing, reference, prompt and threshold identities independently versioned. Frozen 8-bit view inputs avoid the loader's known native-source parity differences; this run does not introduce native crops or gamma views.

The first stats text treatment drops `focus_area`. Measurements are explicitly auxiliary evidence for technical quality; both score fields are still collected to detect aesthetic regressions. Do not include source quality labels, prior grader outputs, reference scores, file names or source-category names in the measurement block. Retain the current photography-scope rule. The uniform-frame gate and other QC rules run in shadow mode, preserving native outputs; no new automatic stats floor is applied.

Freeze a seeded donor mapping for the swapped-stats treatment before calls. Select unique donor family representatives from the same 4,000-image corpus's nonbenchmark background, excluding controlled derivatives. It has no self or same-family matches, with source/encoding/resolution matching where feasible and balanced misleading sharpness directions; record noise and other metric differences. The larger donor frame supports matching without using any quality labels. Retain every donor identity and block hash outside the grading prompt. A response to false measurements indicates sensitivity to supplied evidence, not proof that the image was ignored.

## Reference review and partitions

Reviewers inspect each delivered image individually, with the frozen baseline rubric and anonymous case ID. They do not see student outputs, image-stats values, source labels or other reviewers' scores. References use 1–5 scores in 0.1 increments and brief visible evidence for each dimension, plus eligible/excluded/uncertain scope judgment. Clearly excluded images receive 1.00/1.00; ambiguous origin does not by itself cause a floor.

Freeze the full 500-case reference artifact and its hash before any benchmark scoring request. Nonbenchmark baseline scoring may proceed earlier because those images have no new independent reference labels. Keep the 100 development cases separate from the 400 evaluation cases in all accuracy tables. Existing 72-case studies remain historical/regression context. These reference scores are assistant judgments, not human MOS or verified human ground truth.

Reference inspection may begin on the early candidate selection while embeddings for the remaining background candidates finish. Before the final corpus freeze, review cross-boundary duplicate candidates without access to reference scores. Exclude confirmed benchmark duplicates from the background calibration and donor pools. A confirmed duplicate of a historically scored image requires replacing that benchmark case before inference, preserving any superseded review and recording the objective exclusion reason. Such replacements cannot depend on scores or grader behavior; replacement cases receive a new blind review. Otherwise retain the early selection unchanged.

Reference rows have reported price $0.00. There is no external HTTP request/response for an assistant visual review, so API latency is null with an explicit not-applicable reason. When available, measured visual-review elapsed time is retained in a separate field; it must not be ranked as provider API latency. Missing timings are never imputed as zero.

After the primary reference freeze, independently repeat 50 reference assessments using fresh reviewer context: 10 development, 30 representative and 10 challenge cases selected by a fixed hash ordering (`reference-repeat-20261101:<case_id>`), without using scores. Repeat reviewers see only anonymous images and the same rubric, never primary labels or model results. Record these in a separate reliability artifact and report per-task absolute disagreement and scope agreement. The primary labels remain unchanged; this is assistant repeatability evidence, not human validation or an additional treatment endpoint.

## Runtime, billing and result contract

Store native and policy-adjusted technical/aesthetic scores, validity by task, image/view identity, arm, model/provider identity, raw response and usage, timestamps, end-to-end request latency and price evidence. Measure HTTP end-to-end latency with a monotonic clock immediately before sending through receipt of the full response. It includes upload, network/proxy and server queue/processing time. Local scheduler waits, model startup and downloads are separate. Report concurrency and latency distributions rather than implying a controlled production-speed comparison across different routes.

For the Luna/DeepSeek derived mean, report no fabricated request latency or independent charge; component costs and timings remain separately accessible. Aggregate cost accounting counts actual attempts once, including failures when charged. Unknown costs remain null. For Q-ReAlign Lite every requested displayed per-image price is $0.00, explicitly marked as a reporting override; Runpod infrastructure usage/cost is recorded separately. The Reference price is also $0.00. Keep full precision internally and display both scores to two decimals. Preserve small API prices at sufficient decimal precision to avoid rounding every call to zero.

Pin the Lite checkpoint, question wording, native five-word probability readout and 1+4*score normalization to the previous 72-image Runpod trial. Native scores have no new eligibility floor. Bounded overlapping HTTP submissions may improve transport utilization while preserving the original serial GPU scorer; queue time is included in each request's latency. Record this concurrency change. Stop the resumed trial pod after results have been retrieved or the bounded run limit is reached.

API requests are journaled durably before dispatch, with raw outcomes and immutable payload/image/route hashes for safe recovery. Retry only known pre-serve rejections with a bounded policy; ambiguous transport failures and invalid completed responses require explicit audit. Never repeat a successful or invalid scored result to seek a preferable label. Exact per-route spending caps and request counts belong to the frozen runner configuration. Credentials never enter committed artifacts.

## Analysis and advancement criteria

Report technical and aesthetic MAE, signed bias and rank agreement separately on common valid cases, with combined error as a secondary summary. Evaluate each grader, the fixed 50/50 Luna/DeepSeek mean, and paired treatment differences. Report representative and challenge panels separately, plus development results labeled as such. Use paired source-family bootstrap intervals, preserving all related observations together. These intervals are conditional on assistant labels and do not quantify human judgment uncertainty.

Predeclared practical targets for a stats treatment are technical MAE reduction of at least 0.05 with a paired interval below zero, aesthetic MAE noninferiority within +0.03, and no material new errors in intentional-effects or scope cases. Report uncertainty rather than declaring protected slices unchanged from low-powered comparisons. Control multiple comparisons or treat nonprimary model/slice results as exploratory. The primary treatment comparison is own-stats versus baseline for the Luna/DeepSeek mean on the 300 representative evaluation cases; individual models, swapped-stat effects, challenger comparisons and pooled 400-case evaluation results are secondary. Do not remove a valid paired case merely because an unrelated model lacks a score; report all-model common-case comparisons separately.

An advancement interpretation additionally requires at least 95% of the planned panel to have paired valid scores for each task, with no increase in missing or invalid scores relative to baseline. Report planned, reference-numeric and paired counts explicitly. Smaller subsets may retain descriptive estimates and intervals, but cannot establish treatment readiness. Even adequate coverage and favorable aggregate error require the protected-slice review above.

Before model calls, mark a small descriptive style panel from reference evidence mentioning shallow focus, monochrome, silhouettes or sparse compositions, confirming the visible attributes from the images. Keep all reference-excluded and reference-uncertain images as separate scope panels. These tags do not alter scores or eligibility. For the stats arms, inspect every new scope-floor disagreement and each style-panel case whose absolute technical or aesthetic error increases by at least 0.50 against baseline. Report all tagged-case changes, including improvements; a small clean panel cannot establish noninferiority for those styles in general. Freeze the case tags before viewing grader outputs.

Measure QC precision/recall for large errors (absolute technical error at least 0.75) and yield at a fixed review budget, including random unflagged audits. Test rule performance separately for each grader. Error correlation alone does not validate a review rule.

Use a fixed review budget of 10% of each panel, reserving 20% of those slots for seeded random unflagged audits. Fill the remaining slots from gate, softness and noise flags in the predeclared priority order. Reallocate slots only when one pool is too small; report actual flagged and unflagged coverage and compare with random review at the same total budget.

For controlled ladders, first inspect generation parameters and delivered pixels. Lack of movement in a proxy metric is not sufficient to discard a family. Preserve the all-family analysis and any independently justified verified subset with reasons. Report non-increasing technical ladders, ties, strict inversions and parent-to-severity-3 differences separately. The parent/variant statistic is not PACE's ground-truth-threshold Holistic Override Rate. No aesthetic monotonicity constraint is imposed.

Independently inspect the 100 controlled families without grader outputs. Each family packet shows all four full frames and an identical-coordinate native-resolution detail crop selected from the parent image only; exact individual views remain available. Record each derivative versus its parent and preceding level as clear degradation, not clearly worse, or unjudgeable, with visible evidence. A parent-comparison subset requires all three derivatives to be clearly worse than the parent; a fully verified ladder additionally requires clear worsening at each adjacent step. Report both subset sizes and exclusions, retain the all-family results, and do not replace or change any model score based on this inspection.

Publish per-model/arm completion, schema validity, costs, latency distributions, error slices and visual comparisons. The final report must distinguish all-4,000 agreement/operational measurements from the 500-case assistant-reference comparison. Bulk labels remain experimental until a separate training-approval decision.
